How much Jev 1.13's answers change when the order of the options is shuffled

Each item was asked 10 times. For multiple-choice items the options were put in a new random order every time; Likert and yes/no items were left as they were, so they show how much the answers vary with nothing changed. The measure is total-variation distance between two answer distributions: 0 means identical, 1 means no overlap at all.

Mean distance over all pairs of the 10 repeats, per item and prompt; run of 12–13 Oct 2026. Bands are unequal: finer near zero, where most cells sit.
