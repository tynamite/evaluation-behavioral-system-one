Jev 1.13 and Jev 1.14: the distribution Jev returns against its single answer

Asked for a random number from 1 to 10, 33% of people say 7. Each Jev call returns a probability for every number (solid) and also picks one (outlined); counting the single picks is how a chat model has to be read. The single answers put 99% to 100% of calls on 7. The distribution is the closer of the two to people in all 4 panels. Claude Haiku 5.5, asked 200 times, said 7 every time.

Same scale in every panel. Jev: 100 calls per prompt, run on 15 Oct 2026, options shuffled per call. Claude Haiku 5.5: 100 calls per prompt at temperature 1, run on 15 Oct 2026. People: YouGov, 12–13 Sep 2024, open text box; published percentages normalised. Distance: total variation, 0 (the same) to 1 (no overlap).
