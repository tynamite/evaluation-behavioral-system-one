Dictator game · no instruction · four models

Four System One models' answer distributions in the dictator game, against people's

You are given $10 and decide how much to give an anonymous stranger, who cannot respond. Grey: the share of people giving each amount. Coloured: the probability each model returns for that amount, averaged over 10 calls. People have three peaks, at nothing, half and everything.

Same scale in every panel. Models: mean of 10 returned distributions; Jev run 18 Sep 2026, the others 12 Oct 2026. People: Engel (2011), Experimental Economics 14:583-610, Figure 2; 20,813 dictators in 328 treatments.
