Fifteen models · no instruction vs. told to answer like a typical person

Distance from published human results, with and without the ‘typical person’ instruction

Each point is one model. Distance is the mean absolute difference from the published human result over the 14 measures in the decision-making and economic-game charts, each on a 0–1 scale, so 0 would mean identical to people. Across: no instruction. Up: told to answer like a typical person. Points below the dashed line moved closer to people when told.

Axes zoomed to the data: 0.15–0.55 across, 0.05–0.35 up, not from zero. Models run 12–13 Oct 2026, except Jev 1.14 on 14 Oct 2026; Drex 1.5 and Drex 2.0 on 15 Oct 2026. Jev 1.14 was called through TypeSafe's API directly, the rest through OpenRouter. 14 probability-scale measures (each 0–1), 10 replicates or samples per cell. Distance alone is not a ranking: see the framing figure.
