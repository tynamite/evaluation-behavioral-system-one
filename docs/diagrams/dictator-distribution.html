Jev 1.13's answer distribution in the dictator game

The game: you are given $10 and decide how much to give an anonymous stranger, who cannot respond. Each call to Jev returns a probability for every amount from $0 to $10; the bars average 10 calls. A chat model gives one amount per call, so it would have to be asked many times and its answers counted to estimate the same shape.

Mean of 10 returned distributions, run of 18 Sep 2026. $6 to $10 are pooled into one bar. Human shares: Engel (2011) meta-analysis of dictator-game experiments.
