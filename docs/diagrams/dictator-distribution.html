Dictator game · no instruction

How much people and Jev 1.13 give away in the dictator game

The game: you are given $10 and decide how much to give an anonymous stranger, who cannot respond. People mostly give nothing (36%) or half (17%), and 5% give it all. Jev returns a probability for every amount in a single call: it puts 62% on nothing and 18% on half, and almost none on giving everything. A chat model would have to be asked many times to estimate a shape like this.

People: Engel (2011), Experimental Economics 14:583-610, Figure 2; 20,813 dictators in 328 treatments, bars read from the figure's labels. Jev: mean of 10 returned distributions, no instruction, run of 18 Sep 2026.
