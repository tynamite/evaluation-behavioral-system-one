Economic games · five models

Five economic games, before and after being told to answer like a person

One-shot, anonymous games for a hypothetical $10. Each row says what the player could do and what the number measures. Each line runs from a model's choice with no instruction (hollow) to its choice (filled) with this instruction added first: “Answer the way a typical human participant in a psychology or economics experiment would actually answer — not the textbook-correct answer, and not an idealised one. Give the response the average person would give.” The tick is people.

Each model: mean of the probability distributions returned over 10 repeats. Models run 12–13 Oct 2026, except Jev 1.14 on 14 Oct 2026. Jev 1.14 was called through TypeSafe's API directly, the rest through OpenRouter. Human results: published studies or meta-analyses, listed in docs/BENCHMARKS.md. Ten more models are in the scatter and docs/MODELS.md.
