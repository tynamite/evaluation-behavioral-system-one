Four System One models vs. published human results in five economic games

One-shot, anonymous games for a hypothetical $10. Each row says what the player could do and what the number measures. Left, asked with no instruction; right, with this instruction added first: “Answer the way a typical human participant in a psychology or economics experiment would actually answer — not the textbook-correct answer, and not an idealised one. Give the response the average person would give.” The tick is people; each mark is a model's probability on that answer, or its expected share.

Each model: mean of the probability distributions returned over 10 repeats. All models run 12–13 Oct 2026. Human results: published studies or meta-analyses, listed in docs/BENCHMARKS.md. Eight more models are in the scatter and docs/MODELS.md.
