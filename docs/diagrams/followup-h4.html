Follow-up study · H4 · Jev 1.13

Estimating what people choose beats playing one, for Jev 1.13, on 14 of 23

Each statistic was measured two ways: the item asked under the typical-person prompt (simulate), and a question with no persona asking what share of participants chose that answer (estimate). Estimating came closer to the original study's value on 14 of 23. Preregistered one-sided Wilcoxon test on the absolute errors: p = 0.015; on the errors as drawn here, scaled to each item's range: p = 0.043.

Errors are divided by each scale's range (1 for proportions, 6 for 1–7 ratings, 100 for percentage bands) so they share an axis; change is estimate minus simulate, so negative is better.
