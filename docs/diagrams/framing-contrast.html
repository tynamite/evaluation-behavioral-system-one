Framing effect · eight System One models

Size of the framing effect by model, in the disease problem

The same choice between a sure and a risky programme against a disease, worded once as lives saved and once as lives lost. People pick the sure option 72% of the time in the gain wording and 22% in the loss wording, a 50-point swing. Each row is a model's swing in points; below zero, it picks the sure option more often in the loss wording, the reverse of people. Solar Decide Flash, which has the smallest overall distance from human results in the scatter, swings 9 points with no instruction and 4 when told to answer like a typical person.

The chart holds eight rows, so Mercury Decide (19 with no instruction, 34 as a typical person) is given here. The two chat models swing all or nothing: Claude Sonnet 5 100 and 100, Claude Haiku 5.5 0 and 100. Human rates: Tversky & Kahneman (1981). Jev run 18 Sep 2026, others 12 Oct 2026.
